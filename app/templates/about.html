AI Song Remixer

About This Application

How It Works

🎵 Song Upload

Upload your MP3, WAV, OGG, or M4A audio files to the web app. Each upload is stored with a unique name so every remix is processed separately.

🔊 Audio Processing

The application uses FFmpeg-based audio chains to transform your music directly on your machine:

  • Lo‑Fi: Warm, vintage-style sound with band‑limited highs, gentle pitch drift, bitcrush, and compression
  • Jazz: Mid‑forward tone with smooth reverb, subtle echo layers, and light saturation for a “club” feel
  • Dubstep: Heavy low‑end, wobble modulation, and strong compression for an aggressive, modern sound
  • Vaporwave: Slight pitch‑down, tempo slowdown, chorus, and echo to create a dreamy, nostalgic texture
  • Rock: Presence and midrange boosts, controlled grit, and punchy compression for a more forward mix
⚡ Technology

This application combines a simple web UI with local audio processing:

  • Flask for the backend web server and routes
  • FFmpeg for the actual audio processing and effect chains
  • Bootstrap, custom CSS, and JavaScript for the responsive UI
  • WaveSurfer.js to visualize and control the audio waveform
  • noUiSlider for interactive custom effect sliders
  • Lightweight audio embeddings (numpy + MFCCs) to power the offline “AI Recommend” feature
💾 Download

After processing, you can listen to your transformed song directly in the browser or download it to your device.
